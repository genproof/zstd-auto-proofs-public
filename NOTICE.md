It contains source files of zstd (https://github.com/facebook/zstd, commit
01b7154f1172432f8abe9b3bb9909e14a1176b7d) in lib/, Copyright (c) Meta Platforms, Inc. and affiliates. zstd licenses
them under both its BSD-style license and the GNU General Public License version 2, at your option; LICENSE is
zstd's BSD-style license, the file their headers refer to. Thirteen files of lib/common and lib/decompress are
altered by the scripts in patch/: every changed line carries a comment, and patch/GENPROOF-PATCH.md lists the
changes.

The Clight files src/**/code.v are generated by CompCert's clightgen from the C files; src/zstd/code.v is derived
from the decoder's files in lib/, and the Clight of most specs includes declarations from lib/zstd.h.
harness/corpus.h and env/corpus.h hold frames made by zstd's compressor.

The quickproof/ submodule is QuickProof; its licence is quickproof/LICENSE.
